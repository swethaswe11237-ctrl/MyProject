Under the Hood

How DeepGuard Works

A two-stage deep learning pipeline that catches spatial and temporal forgery artifacts.

01
🎞️

Frame Extraction

OpenCV extracts 20 uniformly spaced frames from your video. Haar Cascade detects and crops faces with padding, falling back to center-crop if no face is found.

02
🧠

CNN Feature Extraction

Each frame passes through EfficientNet-B0, producing a 1280-dim spatial feature vector. A projection layer reduces it to 512-dim for the LSTM.

03
🔁

Bi-LSTM Temporal Analysis

A Bidirectional LSTM (256 hidden, 2 layers) analyzes the feature sequence across all frames, capturing temporal inconsistencies that CNNs alone miss.

04
⚖️

Classification & Verdict

A dropout + linear head outputs a fake probability score. Frames ≥ 50% are flagged as fake. The final verdict aggregates all frame scores.
